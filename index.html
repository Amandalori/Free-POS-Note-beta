About

Free POS Note

Version 4.2 (Free Offline Edition)

A streamlined and efficient Point of Sale (POS) and note-taking system built for modern retail businesses, stores, and individual record keeping.

Features

  • Fast and intuitive sales processing with discount application
  • Interactive Home Dashboard with real-time summary statistics
  • Built-in Chat & Canvas Notes with drawing tools and folder hierarchy
  • Universal HR & Employee management system with attendance and payroll
  • Dual-mode operation: Standard Retail and Bakery/Cafe with expiry tracking
  • Bluetooth POS Thermal Printer & 3rd party Android RawBT support
  • Comprehensive product and category management with barcode and image support
  • Real-time inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
  • In-depth sale history with filtering and export capabilities (PDF/CSV)
  • Fully offline and privacy-friendly using IndexedDB
